Dive Answers

Frequently Asked Dive Questions

Quick answers on licensing, gear, dive depths, and weather safety before you board at Pier 4.

Ask a Question →
Do I need a scuba license for my first ocean dive?

No prior license is required. Our Discover Scuba ($110) and Morning Coral Reef ($85) beginner options include a calm safety briefing and a guided shallow reef dive up to 12–15 meters at a 4:1 maximum diver-to-guide ratio.

What equipment is included in BlueTide Scuba dive trips?

Every trip includes sanitized Aqua Lung or Scubapro regulators, BCD, wetsuit, mask, fins, tanks, weights, dive computer, boat transit from Pier 4 in Goa, and a certified dive guide.

What are the dive depths and boat travel times from Pier 4?

Turtle Bay Garden is 12m deep (10 min boat), Blue Arch Canyon is 18m (18 min boat), SS Horizon Wreck is 24m (25 min boat), and Outer Shelf Drop-Off reaches 30m (32 min boat).

What happens if ocean swell or visibility is poor?

BlueTide Scuba offers free weather rescheduling or a full refund whenever sea swell or underwater visibility falls below our safety standards.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer to try scuba diving?

Basic water comfort is helpful for Discover Scuba, while Open Water Diver certification requires a relaxed 200-meter swim and 10-minute float.

What is the minimum age and health requirement for diving?

Divers must be at least 10 years old and complete a standard medical questionnaire. Avoid flying for at least 18 hours after your final ocean dive.

How far in advance should I reserve a boat slot?

We keep groups to a 4:1 ratio, so booking 2–3 days ahead is recommended. Morning boats depart Pier 4 at 8:30 AM and sunset dives leave late afternoon.
